# Incident response and privacy events

## Declare and contain

Run the ownership validation command from the [operations index](index.md),
then retrieve and contact the configured incident, security, and privacy owners
and open the configured incident system of record. Create a private
incident record and a public-safe Bead reference. Activate the kill switch for
financial integrity, authorization, signer custody, or authoritative-data
uncertainty. Revoke affected browser/API/signer sessions and rotate exposed
service credentials; do not destroy evidence during containment.

## Evidence boundaries

Collect UTC times, release/schema revisions, bounded component status, request
or trace IDs, audit IDs, aggregate amounts/counts, and cryptographic hashes.
Never paste raw Kafka bodies, emails, IPs, authorization headers, OTPs, database
URLs, secret files, or decrypted signer/backup keys into logs, chat, tickets, or
Beads. Restrict any necessary personal evidence to the approved incident store
and apply the configured retention/legal hold.

## Recover and close

Follow the component recovery and rotation runbooks. Validate public readiness,
reconciliation, tenant isolation, authentication abuse controls, consumer
checkpoint/heartbeat, signer funding, and backup verification before reopening.
Record affected data classes and jurisdictions, notification decisions and
owners, recovery time, residual risk, and follow-up Beads. Release a legal hold
only through the retention procedure.

Abort recovery and escalate if scope is unknown, evidence conflicts, an
authoritative fact may be missing, a signing key may be compromised, or required
notifications have no accountable owner.
